Real rapids, real guides

White water rafting means steering a raft down a free-flowing river, and it is one of the best ways to spend a day outdoors. We run guided trips for individuals, families and groups, with a focus on a thrilling and safe experience from launch to take-out.

Every trip includes a certified guide, a safety briefing and all the gear you need: rafts, paddles, life jackets and helmets.

A trip for every group

  • Gentle floats for families and beginners
  • Classic runs with steady rapids and scenery
  • Big water days for experienced paddlers
  • Group outings for friends, teams and events
