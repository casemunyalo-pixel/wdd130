Four Ways to Run the River

Every trip includes an experienced guide, a safety briefing, and all the equipment you need: raft, paddles, life jacket and helmet. Pick the pace that suits your group, then compare the details in the table below.

Gentle Float

A relaxed half-day trip on calm water with small, friendly waves. It is the best place to start if you are new to rafting or traveling with children.

Difficulty
Class I–II
Duration
3 hours
Minimum age
5

Classic Run

Our most popular trip. Steady rapids keep everyone paddling, with calmer pools in between to rest, swim and enjoy the canyon scenery. Lunch on the river bank is included.

Difficulty
Class II–III
Duration
5 hours
Minimum age
8

Big Water Challenge

Fast, powerful rapids for confident paddlers. You will work as a team under your guide's direction to run the biggest drops on the river. Previous rafting experience is recommended.

Difficulty
Class IV
Duration
6 hours
Minimum age
14

Overnight Expedition

Two days on the water with a night camping beside the river. Guides cook dinner and breakfast while you relax by the fire. Tents and sleeping gear are provided.

Difficulty
Class III
Duration
2 days
Minimum age
12
